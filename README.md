# PMVerse 🚀

Link: https://techforpm.vercel.app/

PMVerse is an interactive learning platform built to help aspiring Product Managers understand technical concepts without needing a software engineering background. It simplifies complex topics such as APIs, Databases, Caching, Load Balancers, Authentication, and System Design through visual explanations, real-world analogies, and hands-on simulations.

The platform bridges the gap between business and engineering by providing practical, product-focused learning experiences that prepare users for Product Management interviews and day-to-day collaboration with development teams.

## ✨ Features

* 📚 Interactive modules covering core technical concepts for Product Managers
* 🌐 Visual explanations of APIs, Databases, Load Balancers, Authentication, and more
* 🎯 Domain knowledge simulations for industries such as EdTech and FinTech
* 💻 SQL practice to strengthen data analysis skills
* 🤖 AI-assisted interview preparation and mock PM interviews
* 📝 Product case studies and teardowns
* 📈 Real-world scenarios to improve product thinking and decision-making

## 🎯 Why PMVerse?

Many Product Managers come from non-technical backgrounds and often struggle to understand engineering concepts. PMVerse makes these topics accessible through intuitive visuals, practical examples, and interactive learning experiences, enabling PMs to communicate effectively with engineering teams and make better product decisions.

Whether you're preparing for Product Management interviews, transitioning into Product roles, or looking to strengthen your technical foundation, PMVerse provides a structured, hands-on learning experience.
